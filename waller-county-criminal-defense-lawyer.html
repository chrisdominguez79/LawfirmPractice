Defense in Hempstead and Waller County's Courts

Waller County's courthouse is in Hempstead, the county seat, where the District Court handles felony cases and the County Court at Law handles misdemeanors. It's a smaller county than its neighbors, which can cut both ways: cases can move quickly, and a defendant who doesn't have someone pushing back on their behalf can get lost in that pace just as easily as in a much larger docket.

We take Waller County cases and give them the same individual attention we'd give a case anywhere else, reviewing the stop, the evidence, and the charge itself before deciding on a strategy, rather than treating a smaller county as a smaller case.
