Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Castlerod Criminal Defense,P.L.L.C. ("Castlerod," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or contact our firm through the website.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request a consultation, send an email, call our office, or submit information through a website form.

This information may include your name, telephone number, email address, and information you choose to provide concerning your legal matter.

Certain technical information may also be collected automatically when you use the website, such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, device type, referring website, pages visited, and general website usage information.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through this website may be used to respond to inquiries, communicate with prospective or existing clients, evaluate whether our firm may be able to assist with a legal matter, operate and secure the website, improve website functionality, and comply with applicable legal or professional obligations.

4. Contacting the Firm

Sending information through this website, by email, or through a contact form does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship.

Please avoid sending highly sensitive or confidential information through the website unless and until the firm has confirmed that it is appropriate to do so.

5. Sharing of Information

We may use third-party service providers to assist with website hosting, email, website forms, security, communications, analytics, or other services necessary to operate the website and the firm.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, professional obligations, protect our rights, or protect the safety and security of our website and users.

Castlerod does not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website or services used to operate it may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain website functionality, understand website traffic, improve performance, and protect the website from misuse.

You may be able to control certain cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information associated with the website. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with applicable legal and professional obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate business records.

9. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights regarding personal information maintained about you, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide information concerning legal services and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly use this website for the purpose of collecting personal information from children.

11. Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to websites operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, content, or practices of third-party websites.
